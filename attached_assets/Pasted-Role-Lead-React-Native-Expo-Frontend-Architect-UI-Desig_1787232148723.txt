Role: Lead React Native / Expo Frontend Architect & UI Designer.

Task: Expand our `@workspace/blink` app inside the monorepo from 4 mock screens to 10 complete, state-of-the-art screens, retaining the exact high-end aesthetic, soft pastel palette tokens, typography, and micro-interactions of `BlinkUI.tsx`.

### Design Rules & Visual Language (Must Preserve)
1. Typography & Spacing: Use Google Inter font (`Inter_700Bold`, `Inter_600SemiBold`, `Inter_400Regular`), 18-22px corner radius, clean 14px horizontal padding.
2. Color Palette: Use soft pastel accent tokens (`#f2b5a4`, `#b7d7d0`, `#f4d58b`, `#b6c7e8`, `#bfd9c4`, `#f5bd9e`).
3. Micro-interactions: Incorporate `expo-haptics` (`selectionAsync`, `impactAsync`) on all button taps and card actions.
4. Header Style: Keep the subtle top status eyebrow (e.g. `BENGALURU · 6:42 PM`).
